## GBA-Net: A Multi-Task Medical Image Classification Model Based on Global Biaxial Attention

**IEEE SMC 2025 · Poster · First author and project lead**<br>
February 2025 – July 2025<br>
**Li, H.**, Qiu, G., Zeng, X., & Liu, S.

- **My contribution:** Led research planning, data processing, model development, experimental validation, and manuscript preparation and submission.

<details class="paper-details" markdown="1">
<summary>Methods, contributions &amp; results</summary>

- **Approach:** Developed a multi-task classification framework with dual-channel feature extraction, multi-scale biaxial attention, and adaptive loss optimization to address feature fusion, imbalanced samples, and difficult cases.
